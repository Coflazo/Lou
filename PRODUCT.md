# Lou Product Notes

Lou is a minimal legal workspace for turning playbook knowledge into contract review and controlled playbook updates.

Roles:

- Junior: read playbooks, analyze contracts, use listening mode, submit proposed updates, and comment on findings.
- Senior: Junior permissions plus review, edit, approve or reject proposals, publish commits, and export playbooks.
- Admin: Senior permissions plus company brain and integration credentials.

Demo scope:

- Siemens Mutual NDA sample playbook data is the only seeded legal content.
- Approval is required before a proposal becomes a commit.
- Listening mode prefers SLNG Unified API live voice sessions when configured and always supports pasted transcript fallback.
- Demo voice language focus: English, French, Dutch, and German.
- Pioneer is the synthetic data pipeline for playbook examples; the repo includes a 50-record Pioneer-generated dataset with one preferred position, three fallbacks, one red line, and one deal breaker per record.
