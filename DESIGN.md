# Lou Design

The interface uses a restrained legal-operations palette inspired by Sanzo Wada combination #69:

- Warm Gray `#a1a39a`
- Ink `#111314`
- Paper `#f4f1ea`
- Amber review marks `#e4b84a`
- Green approved marks `#3d6f58`
- Red risk marks `#9c3d36`

The product is page-based rather than dashboard-first. The default density is compact and scannable, with clear role gates and short legal-workflow copy.
